Quran Text
Uthmani Hafs text (QPC Hafs) published by the King Fahd Glorious Quran
Printing Complex, Al-Madinah Al-Munawwarah: https://qurancomplex.gov.sa
Distributed by the Quranic Universal Library (QUL): https://qul.tarteel.ai

The text is bundled unchanged, except that the ayah number ending each
ayah is removed from the text and drawn separately by the app.
Resources/Quran/quran-hafs.json is generated by Scripts/BuildQuranText.swift.

Madani page, juz, and sajdah metadata bundled with this app follow the
standard 604-page Madani Hafs layout used for navigation. Verse display
is flowing text, not a pixel-identical Mushaf page.

Audio
Audio recitations of Sheikh Abdul Rahman Al-Sudais are streamed or
downloaded from the Al Quran Cloud / islamic.network CDN for personal
and educational use. Copyright remains with the reciter.
